# Storyboard — un passeig per la barra

## Creative premise
A real neighborhood bar where familiar food is made with care. The supplied interior reference shows a narrow, long room, a reflective stainless counter on the left, a wall counter and black stools on the right, a patterned tile floor, dark framing, globe lamps and vintage posters. Use these elements to build visual continuity; do not invent the actual floor plan beyond the view shown.

Proposed headline: «El bar de tota la vida. El gust de les coses ben fetes.»
Proposed campaign line: «Bon pa. Bon producte. Bones ganes.»
All headlines, colors and the provisional name may be revised by the owner.

## Media brief
- Best option: film a continuous, stabilized movement along the actual counter once it is ready, and shoot separate close-ups of real dishes with matching warm lighting.
- Preopening option: generate a coherent concept clip from interior references and extra viewpoints. Preserve architecture, seats, tile patterns, material reflections and light positions; do not present generated scenes as documentary imagery of the finished premises. Use owner-approved real photos as soon as available.
- Record or generate approximately 15–25 seconds as a starting point. Test 24–30 fps or an adaptive frame set; choose the sequence size after measuring quality, bandwidth and mobile memory, rather than mandating a huge 4K payload. Use an optimized poster for immediate first paint.
- Shoot mouthwatering food details: cut omelette, tomato-rubbed bread with oil and ham, frankfurt at the grill, simple burger. Avoid excessive styling, unrealistic portions or ingredients not offered.
- A single still allows restrained simulated depth, not a reliable walkthrough of unseen surroundings.

## Content questions for the owner
Confirm brand name and logo, location and opening date, hours, menu and prices, whether coffee/vermouth/other drinks are offered, takeaway and reservations, language preferences, and whether real footage or a preopening concept is available. Never require answers to all of these to build a private prototype; leave unconfirmed data out of published content.
